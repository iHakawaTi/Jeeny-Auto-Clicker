# Keemo Jeeny — Install Guide

## 1. Turn off Play Protect scanning

Play Store → tap your profile picture (top-right) → **Play Protect** → gear icon (top-right) → toggle **OFF** *"Scan apps with Play Protect"*.

## 2. Download the APK

Tap the download link the admin sent you. If your browser warns *"this file may harm your device"*, tap **Download anyway**.

## 3. Allow install from unknown sources

Open the `.apk` from your Downloads folder. When Android says *"For your security…"*:
- Tap **Settings** in the popup
- Turn **ON** *"Allow from this source"*
- Go back and tap **Install**

## 4. Enable "Restricted Settings" for Keemo Jeeny (Android 13+)

**This is the step most people miss. Without it, Accessibility can't be granted.**

Settings → Apps → **Keemo Jeeny** → tap the ⋮ menu (top-right) → *"Allow restricted settings"* → **Allow**.

## 5. Create your account

Open Keemo Jeeny → **Register Now** → fill in name, phone, email, password → **Create Account**. You get a 48-hour free trial immediately.

## 6. Grant permissions

On the Dashboard, flip the Master Switch. The app walks you through, in order:

- **Accessibility** → find *"Keemo Auto-Accept Engine"* → toggle ON → Allow
- **Overlay** → toggle ON *"Display over other apps"*
- **Battery** → Allow when asked to ignore battery optimization
- **Auto-Start** (Xiaomi / Huawei / OPPO / Vivo only) → find Keemo Jeeny → ON

## 7. Go online

- Set **Min Fare** and **Max Pickup** on the Dashboard.
- (Optional) Turn on the **Floating Button** — a draggable green/red toggle you can tap from any app to switch auto-accept on/off.
- Open the Jeeny Driver app and go online. The first matching order gets accepted automatically.

---

**Not working?**

- Accessibility toggle greyed out → you skipped step 4.
- App stops working after a while → redo step 6 Battery + Auto-Start.
- Nothing accepted → double-check Min Fare and Max Pickup. Still stuck? Message the admin on WhatsApp from inside the app.
